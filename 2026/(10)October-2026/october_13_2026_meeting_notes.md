## News & Small Talk
Tonight was a meeting mainly focused on gauging where the world is revolving around Linux.

- [ArtCraft](https://github.com/storytold/artcraft): an Open Source AI copy of Adobe Photoshop
    - Other softwares like Microsoft Office have been cloned too
    - Raises questions on what proprietary software consists of, and how software as a business will evolve.
    - "The open source image editor you already know how to use."
- Playstation 5 mod [PS5SX2](https://github.com/Swordpdf/PS5SX2) allows PS2 games to run on jailbroken consoles
- Let's Encrypt shortens planning to shorten the duration of web certificates from 90 days to 45 days in 2028 [documentation](https://letsencrypt.org/docs/cert-lifetimes/)[Let's Encrypt FAQ](https://letsencrypt.org/docs/faq/#what-ip-addresses-does-let-s-encrypt-use-to-validate-my-web-server)
    - Certbot server/services will need to check in more often to obtain certificates
    - Follows industry plans to shorten all certificates to 47 days by 2029
- [US Space Force announcing military weapons in space](https://www.spaceforce.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4601219/secaf-announces-on-orbit-space-control-weapons/)
